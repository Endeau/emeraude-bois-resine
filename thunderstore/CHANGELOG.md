# Changelog

## 1.3.0 — 2026-11-24
- Resined versions of the 67° plain wood pieces: sloped walls (upright and upside down), beam, wall top, and thatch roof slope, ridge and corners.
- If a copied piece needs something other than wood, only the wood is doubled.
- Every player needs this version.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-24
- Resined versions of the sloped walls (26° and 45°, upright and upside down), the 26° and 45° beams and the thatch roofs (slopes, ridges, inner and outer corners), so a whole plain wood house can share the amber color. The game already spared them from rain wear: for these pieces the resined version is about the look.
- Every player needs this version.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-23
- Resined pieces now cost twice the wood of the original instead of the original recipe plus 1 resin: resin was too slow to farm.
- Pieces already built keep working; deconstructing them now gives back the doubled wood, not the resin.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-23
- New hammer tab *Resined wood*: a resined copy of every plain wood building piece (floors, walls, beams, poles, stairs, ladder, doors, gate…), found automatically at startup.
- Same piece and recipe as the original, plus 1 resin. Resined pieces no longer wear out in the rain or in water; health, support and strength are unchanged.
- Slightly darker, amber wood to tell them apart.
